# Parent & Kid User Guide — Spelling Stars 🌟

Welcome to **Spelling Stars**! This guide explains how parents can set up a weekly spelling list in seconds and how kids can use the flashcards and audio quizzes to ace their Friday spelling test.

---

## Part 1: For Parents — Preparing the Weekly Word List in Seconds

Whenever your daughter brings home a new spelling list from school:

1. **Open the `Words` Tab** (bottom-right icon on the navigation bar).
2. **Create a New Week (All Words at Once):**
   - Tap the blue **`+ New Week`** button in the top-right corner.
   - Choose a fun icon (like `🌈`, `🦄`, `🚀`, or `🐶`) and enter a title (for example, *Week 3: Super Spellers*).
   - In the **All Spelling Words** box, type or paste **all of the week's words at once** — you can put one word per line, or separate them with commas or spaces:
     ```text
     rainbow, umbrella, whisper, promise, elephant, chocolate, calendar, laughter
     ```
   - Watch the green preview box (`✅ 8 words ready`) automatically detect every word as a chip.
   - Tap **`Create (8 Words)`**!
3. **Add More Words to the Current Week Anytime:**
   - Tap **`Add Words`** to paste or type multiple additional words at once into the current week.
   - Or tap **`+ Custom Word`** if you want to add a single word with your own custom definition or family inside-joke example sentence!
4. **Edit or Delete Any Card:**
   - Every prepared card shows the word, syllable breakdown (`be • cause`), definition, example sentence, and the **2 auto-generated look-alike quiz words** (`🎯 Quiz Look-Alikes: becuase, becasue`).
   - Tap the **pencil icon (`✏️`)** on any word card to customize its spelling, syllables, definition, or example sentence, or tap the **trash icon (`🗑️`)** to remove it.

---

## Part 2: For Kids — Studying with 3D Flashcards (`Cards` Tab)

1. **Explore the Front of the Card:**
   - Read the big word in the center and look at the yellow **syllable pill** (`be • cause`) to see how the word breaks into sound chunks.
   - Look at the **colored letter tiles** underneath:
     - **Pink tiles** are vowels (`a, e, i, o, u`).
     - **Blue tiles** are consonants.
2. **Listen to the Audio Buttons:**
   - Tap **`🔊 Say It`** to hear the word pronounced at normal speed.
   - Tap **`🐢 Slow`** to hear the word stretched out slowly.
   - Tap **`🗣️ A-B-C`** to hear the app say the word, spell every letter out loud (`b, e, c, a, u, s, e`), and repeat the word!
3. **Flip the Card in 3D:**
   - Tap anywhere on the card (or tap **`Flip Card`** at the bottom) to flip it over in 3D.
   - On the back, read the meaning and example sentence, or tap **`🔊 Read Word & Sentence`** to hear them read aloud.
4. **Track Your Mastery & Earn Stars:**
   - If the word still feels tricky, tap **`↻ Practice More`**.
   - When you know how to spell it, tap **`✓ I Got It! ⭐`** to mark the word **Mastered** and earn **+2 Stars**!
   - Use the filter chips at the top (**All Words**, **Learning**, **Mastered ⭐**) to focus only on the words you are still learning.

## Part 3: For Kids — Taking the Practice Quizzes (`Quiz` Tab)

Tap the **`Quiz`** tab at the bottom to test yourself with two fun game modes:

### Mode 1: 🎯 Pick Word (Tricky Twins Multiple Choice)
1. **Listen to the Dictation:** The app automatically speaks the word out loud when each question appears.
2. **Replay as Many Times as You Want:**
   - Tap **`🔊 Play Word`** to hear it again.
   - Tap **`🐢 Slow`** to hear it slowly.
   - Tap **`📖 Sentence`** to hear the word used in a sentence.
3. **Spot the Real Spelling:**
   - You will see **3 choices (`A`, `B`, `C`)** — 1 is the real word (`because`) and 2 are sneaky look-alike twins (`becuase`, `becasue`).
   - Tap the spelling you think is right! You'll get instant feedback and earn Stars for every right answer.

### Mode 2: ⌨️ Listen & Type (Full Spelling Dictation)
1. Tap **`⌨️ Listen & Type`** at the top of the Quiz screen.
2. Listen to the word (and tap **`💡 Hint`** if you want to peek at the first and last letters: `b _ _ _ _ _ e`).
3. Type the word on the keyboard (auto-correct is turned off so the keyboard won't cheat or get in your way!) and tap **`Check My Spelling!`**.
4. See a **letter-by-letter color check** showing green tiles for every matching letter and pink tiles for any letter to fix.
